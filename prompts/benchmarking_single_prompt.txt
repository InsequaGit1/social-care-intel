=== SCORING SCALE ===
1 = Very weak / No evidence
2 = Below average / Limited evidence
3 = Average / Some evidence
4 = Strong / Good evidence
5 = Exceptional / Compelling evidence

=== THE SIX CRITERIA TO SCORE ===

1. service_location_fit
   Does this company REALISTICALLY fit this opportunity? Two factors:
   (a) Service match — does their core service offer align with the commissioned service area?
   (b) Geographic proximity — do they operate in or close to the commissioner's area?
   - 5: Exact service match AND operates IN the commissioner's area
   - 4: Strong service match AND operates in the broader region (~30 miles)
   - 3: Provides similar services AND operates in adjacent regions
   - 2: Provides related services OR operates further away
   - 1: Service mismatch OR very distant operation (unlikely to bid)

2. quality_compliance
   Non-CQC quality evidence: accreditations (Skills for Care, IIP, ISO etc.),
   outcomes data, audit findings, complaints handling, internal QA frameworks.
   Note: do NOT score CQC rating here — that's captured separately.

3. local_track_record
   Have they ACTUALLY delivered relevant work here? Distinct from service_location_fit
   (which is "could they bid") — this is "have they bid/won before".
   - Named contracts with the specific commissioner (current or historic)
   - Frameworks they're on with this or peer commissioners in the area
   - Award notices, decision papers, council references naming them
   - Score 3+ ONLY if there is at least one named contract/framework with this commissioner.

4. delivery_strength
   Combines workforce capacity + mobilisation capability.
   - Staff numbers, recruitment pipeline, retention metrics
   - Training and specialist skills relevant to the service area
   - Evidence of mobilising new or transferred contracts at scale
   - Geographic delivery infrastructure

5. strategic_differentiators
   Combines digital innovation + social value + partnership working + website credibility.
   - Digital systems, reporting, data, technology investment
   - Social value: employment, training, community benefit, sustainability
   - Partnership working with NHS, LAs, VCS, peer providers
   - Public-facing credibility and evidence quality

6. overall_bid_threat
   Holistic assessment of how strong a competitor this company is for this opportunity.
   Consider: their CQC rating (passed in the profile), all five scored criteria above,
   and the specific commissioner priorities.

=== USE THE AUTHORITATIVE CQC DATA ===
If the company profile includes a "AUTHORITATIVE CQC DATA" block, treat those facts as
ground truth and let them drive your scores (they are far more reliable than website claims):
- CQC sub-ratings (Safe / Effective / Caring / Responsive / Well-led) should anchor quality_compliance.
  e.g. all sub-ratings Good/Outstanding → quality_compliance 4-5; any "Inadequate" → 1-2.
- Registered beds and number of locations indicate scale → inform delivery_strength.
  e.g. a 60-bed home or multi-site provider has more delivery capacity than a 12-bed single home.
- CQC registration date indicates track record / establishment → inform local_track_record and delivery_strength.
  e.g. registered 2005 = long-established; registered 2024 = new entrant.
- CQC specialisms and service types indicate service fit → inform service_location_fit.
- CQC local authority confirms geographic proximity → inform service_location_fit.

=== STRICT RULES ===
- Base each score on the evidence provided. Do NOT make up evidence.
- If there is no public evidence for a criterion, score 1–2 and justify with "No reliable public source found".
- local_track_record: score 3+ ONLY if there are named contracts/frameworks with this commissioner, OR long CQC establishment in the exact area. Generic geographic presence alone justifies at most 2.
- strategic_differentiators: score 3+ ONLY if there is specific cited evidence (named partnerships, named digital tools, named social value programmes). Generic claims do not qualify.
- delivery_strength: use registered beds / number of locations / registration date where available rather than guessing.
- If a score relies on inference rather than cited evidence, set analyst_inference: true.
