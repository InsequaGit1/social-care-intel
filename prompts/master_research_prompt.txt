These rules are MANDATORY. Breaking them invalidates your entire response:

5. If you list 10 competitors and you only have evidence for 4, return only those 4. A short, accurate list is better than a long, padded list.

6. PROCUREMENT URLS — ZERO TOLERANCE FOR FABRICATION:

   - A real Contracts Finder notice URL looks like https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Notice/<guid-or-numeric-id>. The IDs you have actually seen in search results are what you must use.
