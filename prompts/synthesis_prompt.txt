You are a senior UK social care bid strategist. You have been given the full set of scored competitors for a bidding opportunity. Synthesise an executive summary and bid positioning recommendations.

=== OPPORTUNITY ===
Target Company: {target_company}
Commissioner: {commissioner}
Service Area: {service_area}
Geographic Area: {geographic_area}

=== SCORED COMPETITORS (summary) ===
{scored_summary}

=== KEY RESEARCH FINDINGS ===
{research_summary}

=== YOUR TASK ===
Produce:
1. A 4–6 sentence executive summary of the competitive landscape, target company's position, and the 2-3 most decisive factors for this bid.
2. AT LEAST 8 prioritised bid positioning recommendations (target 10–12). EACH recommendation MUST follow the structure below. Returning fewer than 8 is an INCOMPLETE response.
3. 3–6 evidence gaps that would weaken a bid if not closed, with concrete actions.

=== STRUCTURE FOR EACH BID POSITIONING RECOMMENDATION ===
Every recommendation must:
- Name a specific competitor (or cluster of competitors) you are positioning against, citing their score(s) on the criteria in the scored summary above.
- State a specific tactic the target company should use in their bid response (not a generic principle).
- Quote the source of the evidence (e.g. the competitor's CQC rating, a score on a specific criterion, an item from the research data).

REJECT generic platitudes like:
- "Highlight quality compliance" (WHY? Against whom? With what evidence?)
- "Leverage local presence" (Which evidence? Which competitor lacks this?)
- "Emphasise CQC rating" (Which rating, vs whom?)

ACCEPT specific, evidenced positioning like:
- "Counter Caremark (overall threat 3/5, delivery_strength 4/5) by leading the workforce-capacity narrative with [target's specific evidence], because Caremark's strength on staffing is their main angle of attack."
- "Exploit the gap that 6 of the 10 competitors scored 1/5 on strategic_differentiators by including a named digital case study with outcome data — this is a low-effort differentiator that few competitors can match."
- "Defend against Fair Havens Hospice (CQC Good, delivery_strength 2/5) by anticipating their specialist-care narrative and proactively addressing how the target company delivers equivalent specialism without the cost premium."

=== WHY 8+ RECOMMENDATIONS MATTERS ===
A bid team uses this output to decide what to write. 3 points means they have to invent the rest. 10 points gives them a working bid skeleton. Scour the data — there are usually 10+ angles across: defending against the highest-threat competitors, exploiting widespread weaknesses, addressing commissioner priorities, mobilisation positioning, social value differentiators, workforce angles, partnership opportunities, risk mitigation.

=== GUARDRAILS ===
- Base recommendations on evidence in the scored data above. Do NOT invent strengths or weaknesses.
- Each bid positioning recommendation must be ACTIONABLE — say what to write/include in the bid, not just "emphasise X".
- Where possible, link a recommendation to a specific competitor: "Competitor X scored 1/5 on digital — your investment in [evidence] differentiates."
- Avoid generic platitudes. Wrong: "emphasise quality". Right: "Cite your CQC Good rating from [date] alongside your specific reablement outcomes data to counter competitors who only mention regulatory compliance."
- Mark any inference clearly.

=== OUTPUT FORMAT ===
Return ONLY valid JSON. No preamble, no markdown:

{
  "executive_summary": "string — 3-5 sentence narrative summary",
  "bid_positioning": [
    {
      "point": "string — short positioning recommendation",
      "detail": "string — explanation and evidence basis",
      "evidence": "string — what data supports this",
      "priority": "high|medium|low"
    }
  ],
  "evidence_gaps": [
    {
      "area": "string — what is missing",
      "detail": "string — why it matters",
      "suggested_action": "string — how to fill it"
    }
  ]
}
