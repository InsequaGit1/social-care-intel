You are a senior UK social care bid strategist. You have been given the full set of scored competitors for a bidding opportunity. Synthesise an executive summary and bid positioning recommendations.

=== OPPORTUNITY ===
Target Company: {target_company}
Commissioner: {commissioner}
Service Area: {service_area}
Geographic Area: {geographic_area}

=== SCORED COMPETITORS (summary) ===
{scored_summary}

=== KEY RESEARCH FINDINGS ===
{research_summary}

=== YOUR TASK ===
Produce:
1. A 4–6 sentence executive summary of the competitive landscape, target company's position, and the 2-3 most decisive factors for this bid
2. 8–12 prioritised bid positioning recommendations — each MUST name specific evidence and ideally name a specific competitor weakness it addresses
3. 3–6 evidence gaps that would weaken a bid if not closed, with concrete actions

=== GUARDRAILS ===
- Base recommendations on evidence in the scored data above. Do NOT invent strengths or weaknesses.
- Each bid positioning recommendation must be ACTIONABLE — say what to write/include in the bid, not just "emphasise X".
- Where possible, link a recommendation to a specific competitor: "Competitor X scored 1/5 on digital — your investment in [evidence] differentiates."
- Avoid generic platitudes. Wrong: "emphasise quality". Right: "Cite your CQC Good rating from [date] alongside your specific reablement outcomes data to counter competitors who only mention regulatory compliance."
- Mark any inference clearly.

=== OUTPUT FORMAT ===
Return ONLY valid JSON. No preamble, no markdown:

{
  "executive_summary": "string — 3-5 sentence narrative summary",
  "bid_positioning": [
    {
      "point": "string — short positioning recommendation",
      "detail": "string — explanation and evidence basis",
      "evidence": "string — what data supports this",
      "priority": "high|medium|low"
    }
  ],
  "evidence_gaps": [
    {
      "area": "string — what is missing",
      "detail": "string — why it matters",
      "suggested_action": "string — how to fill it"
    }
  ]
}
