=== SCORING CRITERIA AND DEFINITIONS ===

Score each company on a 1–5 scale for each criterion below.
1 = Very weak / No evidence
2 = Below average / Limited evidence
3 = Average / Some evidence
4 = Strong / Good evidence
5 = Exceptional / Compelling evidence

Criteria:

1. SERVICE_MATCH
   How closely does the company's services match the specific service area and commissioner need?

2. LOCAL_PRESENCE
   Does the company have established operations, staff, or contracts in the geographic area?

3. COMMISSIONER_RELATIONSHIP
   Is there evidence of an existing or historic working relationship with this commissioner?

4. CONTRACT_HISTORY
   What is the quality and relevance of the company's contract history in this or similar service areas?

5. QUALITY_COMPLIANCE
   What is the evidence of quality standards, compliance frameworks, and regulatory good standing?

6. CQC_POSITION
   What is the CQC rating and recent inspection trajectory? (1 = Inadequate/unknown, 5 = Outstanding)

7. WORKFORCE_CAPACITY
   Is there evidence of sufficient, skilled workforce to deliver and mobilise this contract?

8. MOBILISATION_CAPABILITY
   Is there evidence of successfully mobilising new or transferred contracts at relevant scale?

9. DIGITAL_INNOVATION
   Is there evidence of digital systems, data reporting, or innovation relevant to commissioners?

10. SPECIALIST_CAPABILITY
    Does the company have specialist skills, accreditations, or evidence relevant to the service area?

11. SOCIAL_VALUE
    Is there clear, evidenced social value delivery (employment, training, community benefit)?

12. PARTNERSHIP_WORKING
    Is there evidence of partnerships with NHS, local authorities, VCS organisations, or peer providers?

13. WEBSITE_CREDIBILITY
    How credible and evidence-based is the company's public-facing profile?

14. OVERALL_COMPETITOR_STRENGTH
    Holistic assessment of how strong a competitor this company is for this opportunity.
